MLP — 06 May 26: Machine Learning Practice (100 marks)

Transcribed from official practice-paper screenshots (tail pages p146+ plus header range). Q1–Q23 heads in render range; [calc] = verified; [kit] = kit-derived.

Q1 · 6406532125944 · MCQ · 4m

Naive Bayes variant for Gender from Height/Weight/Age (continuous)? Options: CategoricalNB || BernoulliNB || MultinomialNB || GaussianNB Answer [kit]: GaussianNB.

Q2 · 6406532125949 · MCQ · 4m

ARIMA order=(1,1,1) — role of d=1? Options: lag count || differencing count || MA window || forecast horizon Answer [kit]: number of times data is differenced.

Q3 · 6406532125950 · MCQ · 4m

Assertion (precision always > recall) + Reason (recall independent of FN)? Options: both true + explains || both true, no-explain || A true R false || A false R true || both false Answer [kit]: both false (precision priority is problem-dependent; recall depends on FN).

Q4 · 6406532125965 · MCQ · 4m

Pandas filter: (Age>30 & Salary>70000) | (IT & Exp<6) on 6-employee df → included? Options: Sara, Mike, Anna, Tom || Sara, Mike, Anna || Mike, Anna, Tom || Sara, Anna, Tom Answer [kit]: Sara, Mike, Anna, Tom.

Q5 · 6406532125945 · SA · 3m

DummyRegressor(mean) train [10,15,20,25,30], test [35,40] → MSE? Answer [calc]: 312.5.

Q6 · 6406532125946 · MSQ · 5m

AdaBoost(depth-1) vs AdaBoost(depth-3)? Options: adb1 safer (shallow) || more estimators always better || smaller lr reduces train acc || adb2 lower-bias higher-variance Answer [kit]: adb1-safer + adb2-bias-variance.

Q7 · 6406532125947 · MSQ · 5m

KMeans(9 clusters, kmeans++, n_init=10, max_iter=20)? Options: converges<20 ⇒ nearest-centroid guaranteed || k validatable via silhouette/elbow || 20 independent runs || init picks 9 fully-random points Answer [kit]: silhouette/elbow validation only (10 runs not 20; kmeans++ not random; no mapping guarantee).

Q8 · 6406532125948 · MSQ · 5m

PIL/numpy image chain — which FALSE? Options: image_0 has 3 channels || max pixel value is 256 || resize drops channels || convert flips image Answer [kit]: 256-max + resize-channels + flip (all false; max is 255).

Q9 · 6406532125949 · MSQ · 5m

fetch_california pipeline — error lines? (y = data.label / scaler.fit_transform(X_test) / mse(X_test_scaled, y_test)) Options: Line1 || Line2 || Line3 || Line4 Answer [kit]: Line1 (.target not .label) + Line3 (never fit on test) + Line4 (MSE needs y_pred).

Q10 · 6406532125947 · SA · 4m

KNN k=3 on 6-point set → predict([[3,2]])? Answer [calc]: 0.

Q11 · 6406532125948 · SA · 4m

CountVectorizer bigrams (lowercase=False) on 2 docs → array sum? Answer [calc]: 6.

Q12 · 6406532125947 · SA · 4m

f1_score class 1: Y_True 7-ones + Y_Pred mixed (8-list)? Answer [calc]: 0.73 (8/11 = 0.7273).

Q13 · 6406532125952 · SA · 5m

Multiclass (7×0, 6×1, 7×2) → precision class 1? Answer [calc]: 0.5 (4/8).

Q14 · 6406532125966 · MSQ · 3m

Data augmentation techniques? Options: random cropping || normalize to [0,1] || fixed-size resize || small-angle rotation Answer [kit]: random cropping + small rotation (normalize/resize = preprocessing, not augmentation).

Q15 · 6406532125966 · MSQ · 3m

mlp1((5,)relu-adam) vs mlp2((10,5)tanh-sgd) on iris? Options: mlp2 more params || seed changes preds || sgd always beats adam || tanh always beats relu Answer [kit]: more-params + seed-matters.

Q16 · 6406532125954 · MSQ · 4m

TfidfVectorizer truths? Options: smooth_idf=True ⇒ all-doc term IDF=0 || lowercase=True adds lowercase copies || max_features=1000 keeps top-1000 || min_df=3 ignores df<3 Answer [kit]: max_features + min_df (smoothing avoids exact 0; lowercase replaces).

Q17 · 6406532125960 · MSQ · 4m

Employees in departments with avg salary > 65000? Options: groupby.filter(mean>65000) || groupby.apply misaligned || df[Salary > grouped-means] || transform>6500 typo Answer [kit]: groupby('Dept').filter(lambda x: x['Salary'].mean() > 65000).

Q18 · 6406532125963 · MSQ · 4m

FALSE statements? Options: RF uses sampling WITHOUT replacement || Boosting reduces bias sequentially || Bagging reduces bias by averaging || Boosting trains parallel Answer [kit]: all three false-statements (RF = WITH replacement; bagging cuts variance; boosting is sequential).

Q19 · 6406532125955 · MCQ · 3m

LogisticRegression CANNOT do multinomial? Options: liblinear || lbfgs || sag || saga Answer [kit]: liblinear (ovr only).

Q20 · 6406532125962 · MCQ · 3m

metric = 1 − Σ(y−ŷ)²/Σ(y−ȳ)² range? Options: 0–1 || −∞–1 || −1–1 || −∞–∞ Answer [kit]: −infinity to 1 (R²).

Q21 · 6406532125969 · MCQ · 3m

LeaveOneOut + cross_val_score on 5 samples → ? Options: trains 5 times || trains 1 time || code wrong Answer [kit]: trains 5 times.

Q22 · 6406532125968 · MCQ · 3m

Lasso(0.01) vs Lasso(1.0) — correct statements? Options: 2 and 3 || 1 and 2 || 3 and 4 || 1 and 4 Answer [kit]: 1 and 2 (lasso2 smaller coefs; lasso1 fewer zeros).
