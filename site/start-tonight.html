⚠️ 5 EXAM-HALL FACTS (save you 10 marks of silly loss)

  1. Q1 of every paper is worth 0 marks — "Are you sure this is your subject? YES/NO". Click YES. Don't sweat it.
  2. SA (numeric) answers: type ONLY the number. No commas (write 2968750 not 29,68,750). No % symbol when they say "without the percentage symbol". Read the note under EVERY SA question — it tells rounding ([R2] = 2 decimals).
  3. Rounding: "rounded to two decimal places" = normal rounding (2.675 → 2.68). "Without rounding off / truncated" (MLP 2024 paper instruction) = CUT digits (2.679 → 2.67). The note decides — always obey the note.
  4. MSQ: you must tick ALL correct options. Partial marking is rare — a wrong tick can zero the question. The MSQ-PLAYBOOK tells you how many to tick per pattern.
  5. Comprehension (0-mark) parents: they carry the DATA TABLE. Read the table, not the story. The story is always fake ("purely hypothetical"). Only numbers matter.
